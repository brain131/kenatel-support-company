How to Protect Your Computer from Malware

Malware is harmful software that can damage your computer or steal information.

Common Signs of Malware

  • Your computer becomes slow.
  • Unexpected advertisements appear.
  • Unknown applications appear.

How to Protect Your Computer

  1. Install trusted antivirus software.
  2. Keep your computer updated.
  3. Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  4. Download software from trusted websites.
  5. Back up important files.

If you suspect malware, contact IT support for assistance.

Why Is My Internet Slow?

A slow internet connection can affect browsing, downloads and online meetings.

Common Causes

  • Weak Wi-Fi signal.
  • Too many connected devices.
  • Background downloads.
  • Router or internet service problems.

How to Fix Slow Internet

  1. Check that your device is connected to the network.
  2. Move closer to the Wi-Fi router.
  3. Pause unnecessary downloads.
  4. Restart your computer.
  5. Check whether other devices have the same problem.

If the problem continues, contact IT support.

How to Maintain Your Computer

Computer maintenance helps keep your computer working properly and securely.

Maintenance Tips

  1. Keep your software updated.
  2. Clean dust from the computer carefully.
  3. Keep the computer's air vents clear.
  4. Remove unnecessary files when permitted.
  5. Back up important documents.
  6. Use approved antivirus software.
  7. Report unusual noises or overheating.

Important Reminder

Do not open company computers or install unauthorized software without permission.

Why Software Updates Matter

Software updates fix problems, improve performance and help protect your computer from security threats.

Benefits of Updates

  • Fix security weaknesses.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Fix software errors.
  • Improve compatibility with devices.

How to Update Software

  1. Save your work.
  2. Open the software or system update settings.
  3. Check for available updates.
  4. Install approved updates.
  5. Restart your computer if required.

If an update fails, record the error message and contact IT support.

How to Create Strong Passwords

Strong passwords help prevent unauthorized people from accessing your accounts.

Characteristics of Strong Passwords

  • They are long and difficult to guess.
  • They are unique for each account.
  • They do not contain easily guessed personal information.

How to Protect Your Passwords

  1. Use long passwords or passphrases.
  2. Never reuse the same password everywhere.
  3. Use an approved password manager.
  4. Enable multifactor authentication when available.
  5. Never share verification codes with strangers.

If you think your password has been stolen, change it promptly and notify IT support.
